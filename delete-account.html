Delete Your Account

Roots: Quit Addictions & Grow · by FBBF Studio

You can permanently delete your Roots account and all associated data at any time. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Option 1 — Delete your account in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Roots app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm twice.

Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, email us at finifiniconsult@gmail.com from the email address your account is registered with, with the subject "Delete my Roots account". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and login credentials)
  • Your profile (username and display name)
  • All habits you created, including streaks, reset history and savings data
  • Your friend connections and friend requests
  • Your challenge participation

What is kept, and for how long

  • Backups: deleted data may persist in encrypted database backups for up to 30 days before being purged automatically.
  • Purchase records: if you purchased a subscription, the payment records are held by Google Play (and our subscription processor) and are retained as required by tax and accounting law. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription — cancel it in Google Play → Subscriptions.
  • Support emails: if you have emailed us, that correspondence is retained per our Privacy Policy.
