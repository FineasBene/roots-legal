We usually reply within two working days. If you are reporting a problem, telling us your device and whether you are signed in with email, Google or Apple helps a lot.

Common questions

My plant reset and I did not slip

A plant only resets when "I Gave In" is confirmed with a deliberate press. If one reset on its own, email us with the habit name and roughly when it happened — we can look at what your account recorded.

How do I remove a habit?

Long-press its plant in the garden, or open the habit and use the menu in the top right. Deleting a habit removes its history and cannot be undone.

How do I cancel Roots Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by the store you bought from, not by us.

  • Android: Google Play → your profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → Roots
  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Roots

Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep Pro until the period you have already paid for ends.

I paid but Pro is not showing

Open the paywall and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed into Roots with the same account you used when subscribing — a subscription follows your Roots account, not the device.

I subscribed on Android — do I have to pay again on iPhone?

No. Sign in with the same Roots account and tap Restore purchases. Your subscription works on both.

What is free and what needs Pro?

Free covers one habit with the full garden, live timer, savings tracking, all milestones, badges, the breathing exercise and your history. Pro raises the limit to four habits and adds friends, trigger insights, community challenges and custom emoji.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete Account, inside the app. This permanently removes your habits, history and profile. See the account deletion page for details.

Is Roots medical advice?

No. Roots is a self-improvement tracker, not a medical or therapeutic service. For health concerns please speak to a qualified professional, and if you are in crisis contact your local emergency services.
