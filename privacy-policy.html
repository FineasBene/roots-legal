Privacy Policy

Roots: Quit Bad Habits & Grow · Last updated: July 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FBBF Studio ("we", "us", "our"), the data controller, handles your information in the Roots mobile application ("the App"). By creating an account and using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, a username and a display name. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email and basic profile name from Google to create your account.
  • App content you create: the habits you add (name, icon, daily cost, chosen currency), your start dates, streaks, reset history and the savings calculated from this data.
  • Social features (optional): if you add friends, your username, display name and a limited view of your garden (plant growth stage, streak length and estimated savings) become visible to friends you accept. If you join a community challenge, your participation and ranking within that challenge are recorded.
  • Purchase status: whether you have an active subscription. Payments are processed by the app store (Google Play / Apple); we do not receive or store your card or banking details.
  • Technical data: we store an authentication session token on your device (in the device secure storage) so you stay signed in. A copy of your habit data is also cached locally on your device so the App works offline.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or advertising identifiers, and we do not use third-party advertising or tracking, and we do not run any analytics SDKs.

2. Sensitive information

Because Roots helps you quit habits, the habits you choose to track (for example smoking, alcohol or gambling) may reveal information about your health or behaviour. We treat this as sensitive information. You enter it yourself, and we process it only to provide the App's core features to you. If you choose to use the social features, you are explicitly choosing to share the limited garden view described above with friends you approve. You can stop sharing at any time by removing a friend or deleting your account.

4. Where your data is stored & international transfers

Your account and habit data are stored using Supabase (Supabase, Inc.), our backend, authentication and database provider. Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth and, optionally, Google Sign-In. Subscriptions are handled by the app store's billing system.

Depending on the hosting region of our database, your data may be processed on servers located outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses with our provider.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. We share data only with the service providers strictly needed to run the App:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and storage.
  • Google — optional sign-in.
  • Google Play / Apple — subscription billing.
  • Other users — only the limited garden view you choose to share via the social features, and only with friends you approve.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights or users.

6. Account & data deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Account → Delete Account inside the App. This removes your profile, habits, social connections and history from our servers and cannot be undone. To request deletion without using the App, email us at the address below and we will action it.

7. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is removed from our active database. Backups, if any, are overwritten on a rolling basis.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your data is restricted at the database level so that, in general, only you can read and change your own data, and friends can see only the limited garden view you approve. Session tokens are stored in your device's secure storage. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, restrict or delete your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to object to certain processing. You can edit or delete most data directly in the App, or contact us to exercise any of these rights. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Romania, the ANSPDCP).

10. Children

The App is not directed to children. You must be at least 16 years old (or the minimum digital-consent age in your country, if higher) to create an account. We do not knowingly collect data from children under this age; if you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.
